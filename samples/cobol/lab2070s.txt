       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. STYLES.
       DATA DIVISION.
       WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
       01  WS-COMPANY-INFO.
           05  WS-COMPANY-NAME               PIC X(60).
           05  WS-COMPANY-INCEPTION.
               10  WS-INCEPTION-YEAR         PIC X(04).
               10  WS-INCEPTION-MONTH        PIC X(02).
               10  WS-INCEPTION-DAY          PIC X(02).
           05  WS-COMPANY-STANDING           PIC X.
               88 IN-GOOD-STANDING        VALUE "1".
       01  WS-SHOW-STANDING                  PIC X(03).
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
           PERFORM A200-INITIALIZATION.
           MOVE "ACME CORPORATION" TO WS-COMPANY-NAME.
           MOVE "19470415" TO WS-COMPANY-INCEPTION.
           SET IN-GOOD-STANDING TO TRUE.
           PERFORM A300-DISPLAY-COMPANY-INFO.
           STOP RUN.
       A200-INITIALIZATION.
           MOVE SPACES TO WS-COMPANY-INFO WS-SHOW-STANDING.
       A300-DISPLAY-COMPANY-INFO.
           DISPLAY "COMPANY NAME: " WS-COMPANY-NAME.
           DISPLAY "INCEPTION: " WS-INCEPTION-YEAR "-"
                                 WS-INCEPTION-MONTH "-"
                                 WS-INCEPTION-DAY.
           IF IN-GOOD-STANDING
               MOVE "YES" TO WS-SHOW-STANDING
           ELSE
               MOVE "NO" TO WS-SHOW-STANDING.
           DISPLAY "IN GOOD STANDING: " WS-SHOW-STANDING.
