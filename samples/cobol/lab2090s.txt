       Identification Division.
       Program-Id. STYLES.
       Data Division.
       Working-Storage Section.
       01  CompanyInfo.
           05  CompanyName               PIC X(60).
           05  CompanyInception.
               10  InceptionYear         PIC X(04).
               10  InceptionMonth        PIC X(02).
               10  InceptionDay          PIC X(02).
           05  CompanyStanding           PIC X.
               88 InGoodStanding        VALUE "1".
       01  ShowStanding                  PIC X(03).
       Procedure Division.
           PERFORM 1000-INITIALIZATION
           MOVE "ACME CORPORATION" TO CompanyName
           MOVE "19470415" TO CompanyInception
           SET InGoodStanding TO TRUE
           PERFORM 2000-Display-Company-Info
           GOBACK
           .
       1000-Initialization.
           MOVE SPACES TO CompanyInfo ShowStanding
           .
       2000-Display-Company-Info.
           DISPLAY "COMPANY NAME: " CompanyName
           DISPLAY "INCEPTION: " InceptionYear "-"
                                 InceptionMonth "-"
                                 InceptionDay
           IF InGoodStanding
               MOVE "YES" TO ShowStanding
           ELSE
               MOVE "NO" TO ShowStanding
           END-IF
           DISPLAY "IN GOOD STANDING: " ShowStanding
           .
