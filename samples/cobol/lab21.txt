       IDENTIFICATION DIVISION.
       PROGRAM-ID. LAB21.
       DATA DIVISION.
       WORKING-STORAGE SECTION.
       01  FIELD-1                PIC X(30).
       01  FIELD-2                PIC X(30).
       01  I                    PIC 9(2).
       PROCEDURE DIVISION.
           PERFORM INITIALIZE-FIELDS
           MOVE 'ALPHA' TO FIELD-1(18:5)
           PERFORM SHOW-FIELDS
           MOVE SPACE TO FIELD-1(19:1)
           MOVE 'LANX' TO FIELD-1(23:4)
           PERFORM SHOW-FIELDS
           MOVE FIELD-1(18:9) TO FIELD-2(1:9)
           MOVE " WON'T STOP A T-34" TO FIELD-2(10:19)
           PERFORM SHOW-FIELDS
           PERFORM VARYING I FROM 1 BY 1
               UNTIL I > 28 OR FIELD-2(I:3) = "TOP"
           END-PERFORM
           IF I <= 28
               DISPLAY "TOP IS LOCATED AT: " I
           END-IF
           GOBACK
           .
       INITIALIZE-FIELDS.
           MOVE "XXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXXX" TO FIELD-1
           MOVE "OOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOOO" TO FIELD-2
           .
       SHOW-FIELDS.
           DISPLAY "                   1         2         3"
           DISPLAY "          123456789012345678901234567890"
           DISPLAY "FIELD-1: " FIELD-1
           DISPLAY "FIELD-2: " FIELD-2
           DISPLAY SPACE
           .
